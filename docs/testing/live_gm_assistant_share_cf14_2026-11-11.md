## Defects found and fixed during this verification

1. **`owner_id` UUID/str mismatch (fixed, `5c1e1a1`)** — `POST /api/gm/share/publish`
   returned 500 for every caller: the share routes passed `principal.user_id`
   (a `UUID`) into `ShareAgent`, whose `PlayerShareCreate.owner_id` is `str`.
   The hermetic tests could not see it (`FakeMCPClient` skips tool-argument
   Pydantic validation). Fixed with the repo's `str(principal.user_id)`
   convention at all four share call sites.
