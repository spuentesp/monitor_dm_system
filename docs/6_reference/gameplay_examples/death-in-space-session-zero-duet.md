# Death in Space — Session Zero and Prologue Example

## Purpose
A **duet onboarding example** showing randomized character generation, hub/ship creation, and a bleak transition from setup into immediate salvage play.

## Cast
- **Game Master (GM)**
- **Player (Jax)** creating **Silas**, a Chrome scrapper

## Setting
Pre-game rolling, transitioning to a salvage run on the fringes of the **Inauro ring** in the dying **Tenebris system**.

---

## Scene 1 — The Character (Stats and Origin)

**GM:** Welcome to the Tenebris system. The Gem War is over, the universe is collapsing, and you're just trying to scrape by. Grab your dice. In *Death in Space*, your four abilities—**Body, Dexterity, Savvy, and Tech**—are generated by rolling `2d4` and subtracting `5`.

---

## Mechanical Break — Ability Rolls

- **Body:** `3, 2` → `(5 - 5) = 0`
- **Dexterity:** `1, 2` → `(3 - 5) = -2`
- **Savvy:** `4, 3` → `(7 - 5) = +2`
- **Tech:** `4, 4` → `(8 - 5) = +3`

**Player (Jax):** Okay, so I'm incredibly slow and clumsy, but I'm very perceptive and a mechanical genius. I sound like a guy who sits in a chair and fixes things with his mind and a soldering iron.

**GM:** Perfect. Let's roll your Hit Points.

**Player (Jax):** I rolled a **6**. Not bad.

**GM:** Now choose an Origin. You can be a **Carbon**, **Chrome**, **Punk**, **Solpod**, or **Void**.

**Player (Jax):** With that high Tech and low Dex, I'm going with **Chrome**. Mostly cybernetics held together by duct tape. My character's name is **Silas**.

**GM:** For your Background, let's roll on the **Drifter** table. You start with a heavy wrench and a **glass jar containing a glowing, floating, organic eyeball**.

**Player (Jax):** That is disgusting. Perfect.

---

## Scene 2 — Building the Hub (The Ship)

**GM:** Silas needs a home. In this game, your base is called a **Hub**. It can be a stationary station, or a moving ship.

**Player (Jax):** Definitely a **Ship**. I want to be able to run away.

**GM:** Alright, choose a Ship Frame: **Interceptor**, **Freighter**, or **Scavenger**.

**Player (Jax):** **Scavenger**. Silas makes his living tearing apart dead ships. Let's name her the **Iron Centipede**.

**GM:** Great. Now every Hub has a **history**, a **quirk**, and a **current massive problem**.

### Hub Rolls
- **Background:** It used to be a **mobile prison transport**. The brig still has scratch marks on the walls.
- **Quirk:** The life-support system constantly hums an old, distorted lullaby through the vents.
- **Current Problem:** The **Power Generator is failing**. You are operating on emergency batteries. You cannot make jumps, and if you don't find a new fusion coil soon, you will freeze to death in the dark.

**Player (Jax):** That is incredibly bleak. I love it. The Iron Centipede is a flying coffin singing me to sleep.

---

## Scene 3 — The Prologue (Gameplay Start)

**GM:** The math is done. Let's jump in. It's cycle 44 of the standard calendar. You are sitting in the pilot's seat of the **Iron Centipede**. The cockpit is freezing—your breath is pluming in the air because you've had to turn the life support down to 20% to save the emergency battery. The lullaby is humming softly from the vent above your head.

Outside your reinforced glass, the **Inauro asteroid ring** drifts silently. Your proximity alarm suddenly pings. A massive, dead frigate has just drifted out from behind a rock. It has a functioning **fusion coil** signature.

**Player (Jax):** *"Pay dirt."* I wipe the frost off the monitor. *"Hold together, old girl."* I want to maneuver the Centipede close enough to fire our magnetic harpoons and dock with the dead ship's airlock.

**GM:** The frigate is rotating slowly, making docking difficult. Because you are piloting the ship, make a **Dexterity** check. The standard **DR is 12**.

**Player (Jax):** Oh no. My Dexterity modifier is `-2`.

---

## Mechanical Break — Docking Check

**Roll:** `1d20 = 9`

**Modifier:** `-2`

**Result:** `7`

- **Target DR:** `12`
- **Outcome:** failure

**GM:** Because you failed, you gain **1 Void Point**, representing the cosmic bad luck clinging to you. You fire the thrusters, but your sluggish reflexes overcompensate. The **Iron Centipede** slams hard against the frigate's hull. Sparks shower across your viewport. Metal groans. The magnetic clamps engage, but the impact jars something loose in your ship.

The lullaby from the vents abruptly stops. A red warning light flashes: **AIRLOCK SEAL COMPROMISED**. You are docked, but the frigate's atmosphere—or whatever is inside it—is now leaking directly into your ship. What do you do?

- **Strong mood establishment** before the first action
- **Fast fail-forward consequences** immediately after a bad roll
- **A good benchmark for survival-horror onboarding**
