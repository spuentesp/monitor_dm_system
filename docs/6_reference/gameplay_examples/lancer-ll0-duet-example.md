# Lancer — LL0 Duet Session Example

## Purpose
A **License Level 0 onboarding example** showing Lancer's split between **Narrative Play** and **Tactical Mech Combat**, along with pilot setup, Everest loadout, and a clean transition into the first mission.

## Cast
- **Game Master (GM)**
- **Player (Kai)** playing **Rook**, a former pirate turned Union auxiliary pilot

## Setting
Pre-game building, transitioning to a planetary drop over the dust-world of **Creighton**.

---

## Scene 1 — The Pilot (Narrative Play)

**GM:** Welcome to the year 5016u. You are a Lancer, the absolute apex of mechanized cavalry. Since we are starting at License Level 0 (**LL0**), you only have access to standard **GMS (General Massive Systems)** gear. But first, let's build your pilot. Who is Rook outside the cockpit?

**Player (Kai):** Rook is a former Karrakin pirate. He got caught by a Union patrol, but instead of prison, they offered him a contract as a mercenary auxiliary. He's rough around the edges, heavily tattooed, and prefers fixing things with a wrench or a fist.

**GM:** Perfect. That makes your Background **Pirate**. In Narrative play, you don't have stats like Strength or Charisma; you use **Triggers**. You get to apply `+2` to four different Triggers that represent Rook's skillset.

**Player (Kai):** Since he's a pirate brawler and a mechanic, I'll put `+2` in **Threaten**, `+2` in **Fix**, `+2` in **Survive**, and `+2` in **Spot**.

**GM:** Great. When you are outside your mech and try to intimidate someone, you'll roll a `d20`, add your `+2` for Threaten, and if your Pirate background is relevant, I'll give you `+1 Accuracy`—an extra `d6` added to the roll.

**Player (Kai):** Nice. That makes sense. Rook talks like someone who's been on the wrong end of boarding actions before.

**GM:** Exactly. Now, let's build the real star of the show: your mech.

---

## Scene 2 — The Mech (Tactical Play Setup)

**GM:** At LL0, you start with the workhorse of the galaxy: the **GMS Everest**. It's sturdy, reliable, and has the **Hyperspec Fuel Injector** core power, letting you push it to insane speeds once per mission. First, you get 2 points to spend on your mech's **HASE** attributes: **Hull, Agility, Systems, and Engineering**.

**Player (Kai):** Rook likes to get up close and personal, and he needs to survive a beating. I'll put `+1` in **Hull** and `+1` in **Agility**.

**GM:** Solid. Your Everest now has **12 HP**, **9 Evasion**, and moves **5 spaces** per turn. Now, let's arm it. The Everest has three weapon mounts: a **Heavy Mount**, a **Main Mount**, and a **Main/Aux Mount**. What GMS weapons are you strapping to this chassis?

**Player (Kai):**
- **Heavy Mount:** **Heavy Machine Gun** — inaccurate, but brutal damage
- **Main Mount:** **Assault Rifle** for reliable mid-range cover fire
- **Main/Aux Mount:** **Tactical Melee Weapon** and an **Auxiliary Thermal Pistol**

**GM:** Brutal loadout. Finally, you have **6 System Points (SP)** to install systems and gear.

**Player (Kai):**
- `2 SP` on **Turret Drones**
- `2 SP` on a **Type-3 Projected Shield**
- `1 SP` on **Personalizations**
- `1 SP` on a **Custom Paint Job**

**GM:** The math is done. Your callsign is **Rook**, and your GMS Everest is ready for drop. Let's jump into the game.

---

## Scene 3 — The Prologue (Gameplay Start)

**GM:** You are strapped into the crash couch of your Everest, currently housed in the drop-bay of a Union corvette in low orbit above the mining colony of Creighton. Red emergency lights are spinning. Over the comms, your handler says: *"Rook, local pirates have breached the primary fusion plant. They're trying to steal the core. We need you to drop in and secure the perimeter. Drop in 3... 2... 1."*

The floor falls away.

**Player (Kai):** I grip the yokes. I want to angle my descent so I slam down right in front of the fusion plant's main gate, putting myself between the pirates and their escape route.

**GM:** The atmospheric re-entry is brutal. Your chassis shakes violently as you hit the dirt. Dust billows up in a massive cloud. Make a **Spot** check to see if you can track the pirate mechs through the dust before the tactical combat begins.

**Player (Kai):** I'm rolling `1d20`, plus my `+2` for Spot. Since I'm a former pirate, do I know their standard flanking tactics? Can I get an Accuracy die?

**GM:** Absolutely. Because of your Pirate background, you know exactly how these scavengers operate. Add `1d6` to your roll.

---

## Mechanical Break — Narrative Play

**Roll:** `1d20 = 13`

**Accuracy Die:** `1d6 = 4`

**Modifier:** `+2 (Spot)`

**Total:** `19`

- **Success threshold:** `10+`
- **Critical success:** `20+`
- **Outcome:** strong success

**GM:** A 19 is a resounding success. Through the thick dust, your sensors lock onto three heat signatures. Two are standard pirate technicals, but the third is a retrofitted mining mech carrying the fusion core. They haven't noticed your arrival yet. You have the drop on them.

**Player (Kai):** *"Target acquired. Heating up the HMG."* I'm going to initiate tactical combat. I want to spool up the Heavy Machine Gun and unleash a volley right into the lead technical to announce my arrival.

**GM:** We now switch to **Tactical Play**. You have a surprise round. Spin up your weapons, check your heat gauge, and let's roll for initiative.
