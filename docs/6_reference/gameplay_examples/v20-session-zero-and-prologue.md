# Vampire V20 — Session Zero and Prologue Example

## Purpose
A **full character setup transcript** showing concept-first creation, dot allocation, freebie spending, flaws, and a direct transition into play.

## Cast
- **Storyteller (ST)**
- **Player (Alex)** creating **Julian**, a Toreador sculptor in 1990s New York City

## Chronicle Setup
A V20-style *New York by Night* game with a heavy focus on psychology, social identity, and traditional point-buy character creation.

---

## Scene 1 — Concept, Clan, and Archetypes

**ST:** Welcome to New York by Night, V20 style. Let's get the core concept down before we hit the numbers. Who is Julian?

**Player (Alex):** Julian is a high-society sculptor. He's obsessed with perfection but constantly feels like a fraud. He was embraced by the Toreador Clan because his Sire was mesmerized by one of his marble statues.

**ST:** Perfect. In V20, we need to define your psychological anchors: **Nature** and **Demeanor**.

**Player (Alex):** His Demeanor is **Bon Vivant**—he acts like a carefree party boy who loves the high life. But his Nature is **Architect**—deep down, he just wants to create something of lasting, permanent value.

---

## Scene 2 — Attributes (7 / 5 / 3)

**ST:** Every Attribute starts with 1 free dot. You have 7 dots for your Primary category, 5 for Secondary, and 3 for Tertiary.

**Player (Alex):**
- **Social** is Primary: Charisma 4, Manipulation 3, Appearance 3
- **Mental** is Secondary: Perception 3, Intelligence 3, Wits 2
- **Physical** is Tertiary: Strength 2, Dexterity 3, Stamina 1

---

## Scene 3 — Abilities (13 / 9 / 5)

**ST:** Now Abilities. You get 13 dots for Primary, 9 for Secondary, and 5 for Tertiary. No Ability can go higher than 3 dots at this stage.

**Player (Alex):**
- **Skills** as Primary: Crafts (Sculpting) 3, Etiquette 3, Performance 2, Drive 1, Firearms 1, Larceny 1, Stealth 2
- **Talents** as Secondary: Empathy 3, Subterfuge 3, Expression 2, Alertness 1
- **Knowledges** as Tertiary: Academics 2, Finance 2, Occult 1

---

## Scene 4 — Advantages (Disciplines & Backgrounds)

**ST:** Because Julian is a Toreador, you get 3 dots for your Clan Disciplines: **Auspex**, **Celerity**, and **Presence**.

**Player (Alex):** 1 in each.

**ST:** Now you have 5 dots for Backgrounds.

**Player (Alex):** Generation 2, Resources 2, and Retainers 1—a mortal Ghoul named Sarah who manages his art gallery.

---

## Scene 5 — Virtues, Humanity, and Willpower

**ST:** Every vampire has **Conscience**, **Self-Control**, and **Courage**. You get 1 free dot in each plus 7 more dots to distribute.

**Player (Alex):** Conscience 4, Self-Control 3, Courage 3.

**ST:** That gives you **Humanity 7** and starting **Willpower 3**.

---

## Scene 6 — Merits, Flaws, and Freebie Points

**ST:** You have 15 Freebie Points. You can take up to 7 points of Flaws to gain extra Freebies.

**Player (Alex):**
- **Flaws:** Prey Exclusion (1), Phobia — claustrophobia (2)
- **Merit:** Enchanting Voice (2)
- Raise **Presence** to 2
- Raise **Willpower** to 5
- Raise **Appearance** to 4
- Add a dot in **Melee**

---

## Scene 7 — The Prologue

**ST:** It's 10:00 PM on a Friday. You are in your Soho loft. The floor is covered in white marble dust. Your Ghoul, Sarah, knocks on the heavy oak door of your studio. "Julian? The buyer from the Ventrue clan is here. He brought... a gift."

**Player (Alex):** I drop my chisel and wipe the marble dust from my face. I use a Blood Point to instantly heal a small blister on my thumb from the hammer. "A gift? What kind of gift?"

**ST:** Mark off 1 Blood Point, bringing you to 7. Sarah's voice trembles slightly. "It's a young man. He says it's payment for the statue. Julian... the boy looks like he's been drugged. What do you want me to do?"

**Player (Alex):** I freeze. My Nature is an Architect, I want to build things, not destroy innocent people—especially not in my own sanctuary. I want to activate **Auspex 1 (Heightened Senses)** to listen to the boy's heartbeat through the door. I need to know if he's in danger.

**ST:** Activating Heightened Senses doesn't cost blood, but it makes you sensitive to loud noises. Roll **Perception + Awareness**, difficulty 6.

### Mechanical Break

**Roll:** `2, 6, 8, 9`

- **Result:** 3 successes

**ST:** You hear it clearly. The boy's heartbeat is slow, sluggish—definitely heavy sedatives. But beneath that, you hear the heavy, measured footsteps of the Ventrue buyer pacing in your gallery. He isn't here just for art. He's here to test your Humanity. What do you do?
