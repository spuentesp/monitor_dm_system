# Death in Space — Derelict Salvage Example

## Purpose
A **gritty, blue-collar survival scene** showing simple d20 checks, harsh failure consequences, and cosmic pressure.

## Cast
- **Game Master (GM)**
- **Player (Jax)** playing **Rook**, a desperate scrapper and mechanic

## Setting
The *Ozymandias*, a dead mining frigate drifting in a dense asteroid field.

---

## Scene Start

**GM:** The airlock of the *Ozymandias* hisses shut behind you, severing the umbilical tether to your own ship. You're floating in zero-G in a corridor slick with frozen coolant. Your suit's oxygen readout blinks yellow—you have maybe 15 minutes of air left. At the far end of the hall, the ship's primary fusion battery is still glowing faintly. It's a massive payday. But the shadows cast by your shoulder-lamp are stretching in ways they shouldn't, and something is whispering through the static on your internal radio.

**Player (Jax):** I mute my comms. I don't want to hear the Void singing right now. I unholster my pneumatic rivet gun. I want to kick off the bulkhead and drift down the corridor to that battery. I need to extract it fast before whatever is making those shadows finds me.

**GM:** Make a **Dexterity** check to navigate the zero-G environment quickly without bouncing off the floating, frozen debris. The standard **DR is 12**.

**Player (Jax):** My Dexterity modifier is **-1**. I'm built for hauling, not floating.

---

## Mechanical Break 1

**Roll:** `1d20 = 8`

- **Modifier:** `-1`
- **Result:** `7`
- **Outcome:** failure against **DR 12**

**GM:** You push off the wall, but your heavy magnetic boot clips a floating piece of torn grating. You spin out of control and slam hard into a jagged bulkhead. Your suit's integrity alarm starts blaring as a micro-tear vents a burst of your precious oxygen. Your 15 minutes just became 5. Because you failed a roll, you gain **1 Void Point**.

**Player (Jax):** Damn it. The universe hates me.

**GM:** It does. And the noise of your crash echoed loudly through the frozen ship. From the dark maintenance shaft right above you, a pale, multi-jointed limb slithers out. It looks like a human arm, but stretched and wrapped in rusted wire. It reaches down toward your helmet.

**Player (Jax):** "Scrap this." I'm leveling my rivet gun at the mass in the vent. I want to put a spike right through its center and then use the weapon's recoil to push myself back to my airlock.

**GM:** Firing a ranged weapon in this game uses your **Tech** ability, not Dexterity. The creature is lunging, so you need to hit **DR 12** to pin it before it breaches your suit.

**Player (Jax):** My Tech modifier is **+2**. But I cannot afford to miss this. I'm going to spend the **Void Point** I just earned to gain **Advantage** on this attack roll.

**GM:** Alright. You tap into that strange, buzzing cosmic energy that the Void left in your head. Spend the point, roll **2d20**, and take the highest result. Remember, if you use a Void Point for Advantage and still fail, you risk rolling for Void Corruption.

---

## Mechanical Break 2

**Roll:** `2d20 = 4 and 15`

- **Take highest:** `15`
- **Modifier:** `+2 (Tech)`
- **Result:** `17`
- **Outcome:** success against **DR 12**

**GM:** The Void whispers a cold, mathematical trajectory into your mind, guiding your aim perfectly just as the creature drops from the ceiling. You pull the trigger. **THUNK.** A heavy industrial titanium rivet punches straight through its center of mass, pinning it violently against the corridor wall. It thrashes, shrieking out a frequency that blasts straight through your muted radio, but it's stuck.

**Player (Jax):** I don't even look back to see what the rest of it looks like. I hit the manual override on my airlock door and pull myself inside. "I'm out, I'm out, I'm out."

**GM:** The heavy door cycles shut just as the creature tears itself free from the wall, leaving behind a spray of black, oily fluid floating in the zero-G. You're safe inside your own ship. You didn't get the fusion battery, and you barely have any oxygen left in your suit. But you kept your life. And that whisper in the radio static? It sounds just a little bit clearer now.

## What this example is useful for in MONITOR

- **Fast rules explanation**: what stat applies, what the DR is, why
- **Hard failure consequences** that sharply raise the stakes
- **Resource pressure** (oxygen / time / Void Points)
- **Immediate horror escalation** after a failed roll
- **Simple but flavorful mechanical transparency**
