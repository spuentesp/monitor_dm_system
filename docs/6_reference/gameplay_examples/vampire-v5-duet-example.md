# Vampire V5 — Duet Session Example

## Purpose
A **single-player, high-pressure urban scene** focused on Hunger, Disciplines, messy criticals, and the character's struggle against the Beast.

## Cast
- **Storyteller (ST)**
- **Player (Leo)** playing **Julian**, a Brujah neonate embraced three months ago

## Setting
A rainy parking garage in Downtown Seattle, **2:00 AM**.

---

## Scene Start

**ST:** The rain is coming down in sheets, drumming against the concrete roof of the parking garage. You're on the fourth floor, tucked behind a pillar. The smell is overwhelming—wet asphalt, old oil, and the sharp, metallic tang of the city. But beneath that, you smell him.

**Player (Leo):** I'm leaning against my bike, trying to keep my breathing steady, even though I don't need to. I'm checking the cylinder on my revolver. Does the scent tell me how close he is?

**ST:** He's close. You see the headlights of a black sedan crawling up the ramp. It's slow, predatory. As it passes a streetlamp, you see the driver: it's Marcus, the Ghoul you were told was carrying the ledger.

**Player (Leo):** Alright. I want to use Presence (Awe) as he pulls up. I want to step out into the light just as he parks, making myself look like the last person he ever wants to cross, but also someone he's inexplicably drawn to listen to.

**ST:** Great. Give me a roll for Presence + Composure. Since you're at Hunger 2, replace two of those dice with red Hunger Dice.

---

## Mechanical Break 1

**Roll:** `3, 4, 6 (Regular) | 1, 10 (Hunger)`

- **Result:** 1 success
- **Difficulty:** 2
- **Interpretation:** the Awe attempt does not fully land

**ST:** You step out, and the light hits your leather jacket. You look imposing, but the Hunger pangs in your stomach make your expression a bit too feral. Marcus slams on the brakes. He's startled, not awed. He fumbles for something in the passenger seat—looks like a submachine gun.

**Player (Leo):** "Dammit. Not the way I wanted this to go." I'm dropping the revolver—I don't want to kill him yet, I need the info. I'm going to use Celerity (Cat's Grace) to close the gap before he can level the gun. I want to rip the car door open.

**ST:** He's terrified. He's screaming something about "The Prince's orders." Roll Strength + Brawl to yank that door off its hinges or pull him out.

**Player (Leo):** I'm putting my back into it. I'm imagining the blood in his veins right now.

---

## Mechanical Break 2

**Roll:** `7, 8, 9 (Regular) | 10, 10 (Hunger)`

- **Result:** **Messy Critical**
- **Interpretation:** overwhelming success, but the Beast pushes through

**ST:** Oh boy. You don't just open the door. With that Messy Critical, your inner Beast takes the wheel for a split second. You tear the door completely off the frame with a screech of rending metal and toss it twenty feet away. But you don't stop there. You grab Marcus by the throat, and your fangs are already dropping. You've lost the "interrogation" mindset. The Beast just wants to feed.

**Player (Leo):** "Wait, no! I need him alive!" I'm trying to pull back, but I can feel the Hunger 2 screaming at me.

**ST:** The smell of his fear is like a spice. He's blubbering, "Please, the ledger is in the spare tire... just don't kill me!" You have his neck in your hand. His pulse is thumping against your palm like a trapped bird. To stop yourself from draining him right here due to the Messy Crit, you need to spend a Willpower or find a way to satisfy the Beast.

**Player (Leo):** I'll spend the Willpower. I slam him against the steering wheel instead of biting. I'm growling in his face: "The spare tire. Give it to me. NOW. Before I change my mind about your throat."

**ST:** He's shaking so hard he can barely move. He reaches back, fumbling for the latch. You've got what you came for, but the noise of the car door hitting the pavement echoed through the whole garage. You hear a police siren in the distance.

**Player (Leo):** "Time's up." I grab the ledger, hop on my bike, and kick the engine over. "Tell Marcus the Brujah say hello." I'm peeling out of there before the cops hit the ramp.

**ST:** As you roar down the ramp, you check your reflection in the rearview. Your eyes are still blood-red from the frenzy. You got the book, but you left a massive Masquerade breach behind you. The Sheriff is going to have questions...

---

## What this example is useful for in MONITOR

- **Duet pacing** with one player and one GM focus
- **Immediate consequence framing** after partial success or failure
- **Internal pressure** (Hunger / Beast / self-control) influencing narration
- **Mechanical transparency** where the narration clearly reflects the roll result
- **Scene escalation** from stealthy retrieval → violent complication → escape cost
