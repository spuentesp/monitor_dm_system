# Freeform Roleplay — Vampire: The Masquerade Duet Example

## Purpose
A **rules-light, fully collaborative narrative example** showing one-on-one freeform roleplay without dice, character sheets, or formal GM structure.

## Style Notes
In this format:
- there are **no dice**
- there are **no strict turns or initiative systems**
- there may be **no formal GM at all**
- the scene advances through **shared narration, dialogue, and reaction**
- the main social rule is **no godmodding**

This makes it useful as a baseline example for **pure conversational storytelling**.

---

## Style 1 — “Asterisk” RP (Casual / Chat Style)

Actions are usually wrapped in `*asterisks*`, while dialogue is written normally.

### Scene
A rain-soaked alley in a *Vampire: The Masquerade* setting.

**Player 1 (Elias):** *leans against the brick wall of the alleyway, pulling his coat collar up to block the freezing rain. He checks his watch, tapping his foot impatiently.* Where is this guy...

**Player 2 (Julian):** *steps out of the heavy fog, seemingly appearing out of thin air. He isn't shivering at all. He smooths out his expensive silk tie and smirks.* Right here, Elias. Did you bring the ledger?

**Player 1 (Elias):** *jumps slightly, his hand instinctively dropping to the heavy flashlight on his belt.* Yeah, I have it. *pulls a small, waterlogged black notebook from his coat pocket but holds it tightly against his chest.* But the price went up. The Prince's hounds are looking for this.

**Player 2 (Julian):** *sighs dramatically, rolling his eyes as he takes a slow step forward. His voice drops an octave, becoming dangerously soft.* The price is exactly what we agreed upon. Hand it over, Elias, before I lose my temper. *his eyes flash a predatory, unnatural gold for a split second.*

**Player 1 (Elias):** *takes a step back, hitting the brick wall behind him. He swallows hard, holding the book out.* Fine. Take it. Just keep me out of the local politics.

## Style 2 — “Literate” RP (Paragraph / Novel Style)

This format reads more like a co-written novel, with longer prose and interior tone.

### Scene
The same exchange, written in a more literary style.

**Player 1 (Elias):**
Elias leaned heavily against the cold brick of the alleyway, shivering as the Chicago rain soaked through his coat. He kept glancing at his watch, the rhythmic ticking the only sound besides the downpour. He hated meeting vampires. He hated that he was working for them even more. *"Where is this guy..."* he muttered under his breath, his hand resting nervously on the heavy flashlight at his belt.

**Player 2 (Julian):**
The shadows at the end of the alley seemed to stretch and detach themselves from the wall. Julian stepped forward, his footsteps completely silent on the wet pavement. The rain didn't seem to bother him; in fact, he looked perfectly immaculate in his tailored suit. *"Right here, Elias,"* Julian said, his voice smooth and dripping with casual arrogance. He offered a slight, mocking bow. *"Did you bring the ledger?"*

**Player 1 (Elias):**
Elias jumped, his heart spiking in his chest. He hated how they moved. He slowly pulled the black, waterlogged notebook from his inner pocket, but he didn't hand it over just yet. He squeezed it tightly to his chest. *"Yeah, I have it,"* Elias said, trying to keep his voice from shaking. *"But the price went up. The Prince's hounds were sniffing around my apartment last night. I need hazard pay."*

**Player 2 (Julian):**
Julian stopped walking. The polite smile vanished from his face, replaced by a cold, dead stare that reminded Elias exactly what kind of monster he was dealing with. Julian didn't blink. *"The price,"* Julian whispered, the sound carrying effortlessly over the heavy rain, *"is exactly what we agreed upon. Hand it over, Elias. Now."*

## Core Rule — No Godmodding

Even in fully freeform play, one rule matters: **do not dictate the outcome of another player's character without giving them a chance to respond**.

### Bad / Godmodding

`*Julian steps forward, grabs the book out of Elias's hands, and punches Elias in the face so hard he passes out.*`

This forces the other character's outcome with no room for reaction.

### Good / Fair Play

`*Julian lunges forward, attempting to snatch the book from Elias's hands and throw a punch at his jaw.*`

This preserves the other player's agency. They can decide whether Elias dodges, gets hit, drops the book, or counters.
