## 4. Use Cases and Fixes (Prioritised)

### UC-GM-1 — Consent-before-roll character creation

**Current:** `_maybe_do_char_creation()` calls `gsr.roll_character()` on session start,
unconditionally, and injects the result into the opening message.

**Required behaviour:**
1. GM opens with a scene description (who/where you are fictionally)
2. GM offers to roll attributes, describes the method, and asks if player wants to roll
3. Player says yes / rolls in the physical world / or describes their character conceptually
4. Only *then* does the system generate and display a stat block

**Fix surface:** `_maybe_do_char_creation` — change from auto-roll to a GM offer.
Character sheet generation should be gated by a player confirmation message.

**Session-phase flag needed:** `session["phase"]` ∈ `"pre_play" | "char_creation" | "active"`.
`send_message` should route `pre_play` turns into a light intent-classifier
before invoking the full SceneLoop.

---

### UC-GM-2 — Diegetic session opening (no metadata leakage)

**Current:** System banner announces mode, tone, system label, world engine status
as bullet points. Then GM intro repeats the same information.

**Required behaviour:**
The game context (system, tone, setting) must be woven *into* the fiction.

*Bad:* "Session started in Autonomous GM mode (dramatic tone). System: Death In Space Core Rules."

*Good:* "The Iron Ring station hangs in the dark, a rusted knuckle around the moon Inauro.
The void static hums through every speaker. You are here — and you already know the universe
is dying. Tell me who you are."

**Fix surface:**
- Remove the system-metadata system message for play modes (keep for world_architect only)
- Pre-generate an in-fiction opening line from the axiom/entity context instead
- The `_stitch_gm_opening` function (added last session) already strips the Welcome banner
  but the diegetic opening text still needs to lean on LoreFacts + Entity descriptions,
  not just Axiom statements

---

### UC-GM-3 — One open question, not a form

**Current:** Was a three-question bullet list. Fixed to "Tell me — who are you,
and where does your story begin?" — but this is still two questions.

**Required behaviour:** One question. The GM's art is choosing the *right* single question.
Different session types, tones, and systems call for different opening questions:

| Tone | System example | Good opening question |
|------|---------------|-----------------------|
| Dramatic | VtM | "You are standing outside the Elysium door for the first time. What do you want from tonight?" |
| Grim | Death in Space | "What broke, and which one of your crew died fixing it?" |
| Heroic | High fantasy | "They say there's a price on your head. What did you do?" |
| Sandbox | OSR | "The city gate closes at dusk. You're already inside. Why?" |

**Fix surface:** The opening invitation should be generated by a small DSPy signature
(or an LLM call inside `_stitch_gm_opening`) that takes the system axioms + tone
and produces a single, evocative, in-fiction opening question.

---

### UC-GM-4 — OOC question recognition

**Current:** "what can i be? what type of character?" went to `_run_scene_turn` →
threw an exception → returned "MONITOR could not start the live GM loop yet."

**Required behaviour:**
Questions like "what types of characters can I play?", "how does combat work?",
"what does DEX do?" are out-of-character (OOC) information requests.
They must be handled *outside* the Resolver/Narrator pipeline.

Real GM response to "what can I be?": describe two or three character types that
fit the fiction, say which mechanics distinguish them, and return the invitation to choose.

**Fix surface:**
- Add OOC intent detection in the pre-play routing in `send_message`
- For `pre_play` phase: route to a lightweight `_handle_ooc_question(session, text)`
  that queries the game system schema and entity archetype list, then composes
  a natural-language answer without rolling dice or running the SceneLoop

---

### UC-GM-5 — Graceful failure / ambient GM stalling

**Current:** SceneLoop exceptions become `"MONITOR could not start the live GM loop yet."` or
(after last session's fix) `"*(The GM is gathering their thoughts — try again in a moment.)*"`.

The italic-asterisk format signals the system to the player.

**Required behaviour:**
- Failures during `pre_play` phase should produce a GM response that re-engages the player:
  *"The static rises — tell me again, who are you?"* (re-prompts the opening question)
- Failures during `active` play should describe a scene beat:
  *"The moment hangs. A distant clang echoes through the corridor. What do you do?"*
- The error text must never contain the words "loop", "engine", "binding", "agents"

**Fix surface:** `_run_scene_turn` exception handler — phase-aware ambient responses.

### UC-GM-6 — Dice rolls are collaborative, not automatic

**Current:** The Resolver rolls dice for every player action that triggers Branch 3,
regardless of context. A player typing "I look around the cargo bay" gets a dice roll.

**TTRPG craft principle (DiS, VtM20, PbtA):**
- Only roll when *failure would be interesting* and the *outcome is uncertain*
- Never roll for information the player's character would automatically have
- Never roll for social interaction that doesn't have meaningful stakes
- The GM *proposes* a roll: "that sounds risky — roll Body for me"
- Then the player *makes* the roll (or accepts the GM's narration without one)

**Required behaviour:**
The Resolver should classify the action in three categories:
1. `"trivial"` — no roll (automatic success, GM narrates freely)
2. `"propose_roll"` — GM describes the stakes, names the stat, invites the player to roll
3. `"contested"` — roll happens immediately (combat initiative, trap triggers, etc.)

For `"propose_roll"` actions, MONITOR should say *"Roll your Body stat for me"* and
wait for the player to confirm (or ask the system to roll). The dice should not
fire silently behind a narration.

**Fix surface:**
- Add `"propose_roll"` resolution_type to Resolver
- Narrator should output a roll invitation when `resolution_type == "propose_roll"`
- Frontend needs a dice confirmation widget that sends back `"/roll BDY"` or similar

### UC-GM-7 — Narrator persona and tonal consistency

**Current:** `NarratorSignature` has no persona instruction. The LLM produces
generic RPG prose. Two VtM sessions and two DiS sessions will read identically.

**Required behaviour:**
The Narrator must adopt the setting's voice:
- DiS: terse, industrial, cosmic-dread, second-person present tense
- VtM: baroque, political, melancholy, slow burn
- High fantasy: elevated, mythic, third-person close
- Horror: dread through omission, short sentences, sensory wrong notes

**Fix surface:**
- Add a `tone_context` InputField to `NarratorSignature`
- Include the system's extracted `axioms` (world-truths) and `tone` label in that field
- Add a short system-prompt-style preamble to `NarratorModule.forward()` that sets
  the GM as a specific *type* of narrator for this session
- Vary paragraph length guidance: "1-2 short paragraphs for terse settings, 2-4 for
  baroque" — not always "2-4 paragraphs"

### UC-GM-8 — Scene-opening beats from lore, not just axioms

**Current:** `_fetch_opening_hook` queries only `Axiom` nodes.
Axioms are ontological truths ("The universe is collapsing") — they do not read
as scene-setting prose.

**Required behaviour:**
A scene opener should blend:
1. A *location* from Neo4j `Entity` nodes (`entity_type: location`)  
2. One or two *lore facts* about that location or the active factions/characters
3. Sensory language ("the smell of recycled air", "the flicker of a bad panel")
4. The implicit threat or tension (drawn from the game system's axioms/tone)

**Fix surface:**
- `_fetch_opening_hook` should also query `LoreFact` and `Entity[type=location]` nodes
- Pass the raw results to a small DSPy module that writes one evocative paragraph
  (not a list of italicized sentences joined with spaces — the current output)
- Fallback for empty DB: generate from game system tone and core mechanic alone

### UC-GM-9 — Session phase state machine

**Problem:** The system has no concept of where in the arc the conversation is.
The player could be in any of:

- `awaiting_character` — player has not yet described who they are
- `char_creation` — player is building/confirming a character
- `active_play` — scene is live, turns are in-fiction
- `ooc` — player has stepped outside the fiction (rules question, meta-command)
- `scene_end` — GM is wrapping up a scene, canonizing

Currently all of these route through `_run_scene_turn`, which runs the full
SceneLoop even when it can't possibly work (no character yet).

**Required behaviour:**
```
send_message logic:
  if session.phase == "awaiting_character":
      → _handle_character_definition(session, msg)
  elif session.phase == "char_creation":
      → _handle_char_creation_response(session, msg)
  elif _is_ooc(msg):
      → _handle_ooc(session, msg)
  else:
      → _run_scene_turn(session, msg)
```

**Fix surface:** Add `phase` to session document; update `create_session` to set
`phase = "awaiting_character"` for new play sessions; update `send_message` to
implement the routing above.

### UC-GM-10 — Character definition without mandatory mechanics

**Current:** Character creation is mechanics-first (stat rolls appear before fiction).

**TTRPG best practice (VtM20 "Prelude", Dungeon World):**
Start with the *fiction* of the character — who they are, what they want, what they fear —
and let the mechanics follow from that or be chosen *after* as a reflection.

**Four-step natural creation flow:**
1. **GM sets the scene:** Brief in-world location, situation, sensory beat
2. **GM asks the single opening question** (UC-GM-3 above): "Who are you?"
3. **Player describes their character in natural language**
4. **GM offers to ground that in mechanics:** "For [system], that sounds like high Body but
   low Savvy. Want me to suggest a stat spread based on that, or roll from scratch?"

Step 4 is the *first* time mechanics are mentioned. The player then chooses:
- Accept the suggested spread
- Roll random with the system's method
- Custom-assign within constraints
- Skip stats entirely (narrative-only mode)

**Fix surface:**
- `_maybe_do_char_creation` should return nothing — removed from the opening flow
- After the player's first response is received (`phase == "awaiting_character"`),
  the `_handle_character_definition` handler reads the player's text, extracts
  a character concept, and *then* offers the mechanical options
- The stat roll (if chosen) becomes turn 2 of the conversation

### UC-GM-11 — NPC answers during pre-play ("what can I be?")

The screenshot shows: the player asked "what can I be? what type of character?"
and got a technical failure response. This is the most visible single failure.

Beyond the OOC routing fix (UC-GM-4), the response quality matters:

*Bad:* "Characters in Death in Space have BDY, DEX, SVY, TEC stats."

*Good:* "Out here in the Tenebris system, most crews are a mix of the desperate and the
damned. Some keep their bodies sharp — haulers, fighters, those who work the hull.
Others live by their reflexes, sliding through maintenance shafts or piloting rusted
freighters through dead space. There are the silver-tongued, who survive on charm and
fabrication, and the technical minds who keep everything from dissolving into void.
What pulls at you?"

The answer draws on the *game system entity archetypes* extracted from the manual,
not the attribute abbreviations. It is entirely in-fiction.

### UC-GM-12 — Dice results presented as GM narration, not a log line

**Current (from screenshot):**
```
Rolled attributes: BDY (Body): +10  2d6(5,5) = 10
```

This is a debugging line, not a GM moment.

**Required behaviour:**
If dice are rolled, the result is narrated:
*"Fate's a cold thing out here. You've got the bulk of a former dockworker and the
hands of someone who's fixed too many broken systems — not delicate, but steady.
Socially? You get by. That's something."*

The raw numbers appear in a collapsed or secondary block (or a dedicated character
sheet panel in the UI), not inline with the GM prose.

**Fix surface:**
- `format_character_sheet` stat block is for the UI character panel, not in-flow prose
- When stats are generated, the Narrator should narrate them in character voice
- This means routing the roll result through a DSPy micro-module
  `CharacterInterpretationSignature` that converts stats → character voice description
